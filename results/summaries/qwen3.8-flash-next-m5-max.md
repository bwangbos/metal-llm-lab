## Interpretation and limits

The repeated general-purpose gray zone is approximately 29,000–30,000 effective tokens. Use MTP below 28K, prefer no-MTP at or above 30K, and favor no-MTP in the gray zone when repeatability matters.

Many same-input greedy requests differed between MTP and no-MTP, and repeated requests within one mode occasionally differed despite the recorded deterministic settings. Only rows explicitly marked byte-identical should be treated as such. A visible-string grammar was rejected as a compute-path control because identical bytes can use different BPE segmentations and completion-token counts.

These measurements characterize operational throughput on this workload and machine. Dynamic per-request MTP selection remains a documented direction, not an implemented feature.
